4.5 Scoring Scale

Rating Definition Score
EXCELLENT Respondent demonstrates exceptional ability, understanding, experience and skills. Proposal identifies factors offering potential added value with supporting evidence. 9-10
GOOD Respondent demonstrates above average ability, understanding, experience and skills. Proposal identifies minor additional benefits with supporting evidence. 7-8
ACCEPTABLE Respondent demonstrates ability to meet criteria with supporting evidence. 5-6
RESERVATIONS Satisfies only minimum of criteria but not all. Reservations about adequate meeting of criteria. Little supporting evidence. 3-4
SERIOUS RESERVATIONS Extremely limited or no supporting evidence. Minimum effort made to meet criteria. 1-2
UNACCEPTABLE Does not comply or meet criteria at all. Insufficient information to demonstrate criteria. 0

Section 5: Pricing Information

Instructions for Buyer: Make clear what level of detail required in pricing. Provide Pricing Schedule to structure pricing information.

Requirements:

  1. Respondents must use Pricing Schedule provided.
  2. Pricing Schedule must show breakdown of all costs, fees, expenses, charges.
  3. Where price based on fee rates, specify all rates (hourly/daily).
  4. Respondents must show how they will manage risks and contingencies.
  5. Respondents must document all assumptions and dependencies affecting pricing.
  6. Respondents must tender prices in NZ$ unless otherwise agreed.
  7. Respondents may submit different pricing approach but must also submit conforming Pricing Schedule.
  8. For joint Proposals, Pricing Schedule must include all costs from all Respondents.
