Section 1: Introduction

Definitions

Agency: An office, department, agency, institution of higher education, association, society or other body in state government created or authorized to be created by the constitution or any law, which is entitled to expend moneys appropriated by law, including the legislature and the courts, but not including an authority.

Authorized Users: Includes any state agency, university campus, or other state or local public body authorized to use contracts.

Bid: A price quotation specifically given to a prospective purchaser by a prospective seller; a bid is an offer to sell.

Bidder: A person or firm that responds to this Request for Bid.

Commodity: A tangible article of trade or item of merchandise; goods, products, materials, supplies, or finished products.

Contract: The written agreement between the successful bidder and the state covering the commodities and services to be performed.

Contract Administrator: The agency employee responsible for oversight of the implementation, administration, and completion of the contract.

Contractor: The person or entity that has been awarded the contract.

Disabled Veteran Owned Business: A business that has been certified by the Department of Administration.

Minority Owned Business Enterprise: A business that has been certified by the Department of Administration.

Municipality: Any Wisconsin county, city, village, town, school district, or other public body having authority to award public contracts.

Notice of Intent to Award: A writing issued by the procuring agency notifying all bidders of intent to award.

Purchasing Card: A charge card issued to authorized state employees.

Procurement Specialist: The individual responsible for overseeing and administering the procurement process.

Procuring Agency: The state agency conducting the purchasing transactions.

Services: An intangible product, including actions, recommendations, plans, research, customizations, modifications, documentation, and maintenance.

Specification: A descriptive statement of the physical, functional and performance characteristics required.

  • Contractor notice: 120 calendar days notice

  • Refund: State entitled to refund for goods/services paid but not received.

  • Assignment for benefit of creditors

  • Delinquent tax liability
  • Failure to submit non-discrimination plan
  • Failure to follow non-discrimination requirements
  • Federal debarment
  • Exclusion from federal contracts
  • Failure to maintain insurance, permits, licenses
  • Failure to maintain confidentiality
